Processing Power: The Effect of Data Centers on Wholesale Electricity Markets

With Owen Kay and Reid Taylor

Data centers are reversing two decades of flat U.S. electricity demand. Using an hourly, unit-level dispatch model of 23 regional markets linked by constrained transmission, we estimate that data centers raised average wholesale prices 6.8 percent nationally and 16.5 percent in Northern Virginia in 2024–2025. Under our preferred scenario, the national effect reaches 13.5 percent by 2028. Trade raises prices even in regions hosting few data centers. Roughly half the increase in generator payments is a transfer from consumers to inframarginal generation. We also estimate effects on carbon emissions and derive conditions under which load growth lowers retail rates.
